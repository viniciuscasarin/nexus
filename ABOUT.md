# About Nexus

**Nexus** is an intelligent, AI-powered job application tracker and resume optimization tool designed to give candidates an edge in today's competitive job market.

## The Problem

Managing job applications across multiple platforms (LinkedIn, Indeed, company websites) often becomes chaotic. Tracking which version of a resume was sent to which company, what stage each application is in, and tailoring resumes to match specific job descriptions is a time-consuming and manual process.

## The Solution

Nexus solves this by providing a unified platform where you can:

1. **Track Applications**: A visual Kanban board allows you to monitor the status of every job application, from "Interested" to "Offer".
2. **Analyze Job Descriptions**: Paste a job description and let Nexus's AI engine (powered by models like Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic) analyze it to extract key requirements, skills, and evaluate how well your profile matches the role.
3. **Tailor Resumes**: Automatically generate a customized version of your resume that highlights your most relevant experiences and skills based on the specific job description, increasing your chances of getting past ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) and catching a recruiter's eye.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend/Framework**: Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui
- **Backend/Database**: Prisma ORM, SQLite
- **AI Integration**: Vercel AI SDK (@ai-sdk) integrating with Google Gemini, OpenAI, and Anthropic.
- **Drag & Drop**: @dnd-kit for the Kanban board experience.

## Goal

The ultimate goal of Nexus is to reduce the friction and anxiety of job hunting by automating the repetitive parts of the process and providing AI-driven insights, allowing candidates to focus on what matters most: preparing for interviews and showcasing their true potential.
